## 2026-08-14 - Accessible Emoji-only Floating Widgets in Dark-themed Glassmorphism
**Learning:** Emoji-only or icon-only floating interactive elements (like custom contact widgets) on highly saturated dark/neon backgrounds lack visible focus states by default and are completely unlabelled for screen reader users. Simply wrapping them in descriptive aria-labels and using clear Tailwind focus-visible ring styles (such as `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-purple-400 focus-visible:outline-none`) significantly increases accessibility compliance without compromising the aesthetics of the dark-mode/neon design.
**Action:** Always verify keyboard tabbing navigability and ensure every emoji-only or icon-only interactive anchor or button has explicit focus indicators and descriptive `aria-label` screen reader text.
